# Research governance

## Evidence tiers

Label claims separately as chemical plausibility, preclinical evidence, human observational evidence, or prospective clinical evidence. A lower tier must never be presented as a clinical finding.

## Candidate-record minimum fields

- medicine and unambiguous identifiers
- target or mechanism and source
- pathway link and source
- tumour and normal-tissue context
- evidence tier and model/population
- limitations, contradictions, and safety/interaction context
- next validation step

## Literature

Citation count is discovery metadata, not a quality score. Check bibliographic identity, publication type, correction or retraction status, model, cohort/subtype, metabolic process, and limitations before citing a paper as evidence.
