# Data and privacy

Only public, appropriately licensed, de-identified cohort-level data may be described or processed in an open workflow. Do not upload or commit:

- germline or other controlled-access data;
- patient identifiers, records, or free-text clinical notes;
- credentials, API keys, or private correspondence;
- unpublished or confidential research results.

Controlled-access repositories may be linked with their formal access requirements, but access must remain with authorized institutions and researchers. Public-form users must be reminded not to provide personal health information.
